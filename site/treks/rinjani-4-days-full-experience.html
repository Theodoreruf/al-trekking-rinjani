Overview

Everything from the 3-day crossing, at a gentler pace. The extra night camping beside Segara Anak lake gives you time to rest your legs in the hot springs, explore the caves and watch the stars from inside a volcano.

Highlights

  • Summit sunrise at 3,726 m
  • A full night camping on the shore of Segara Anak
  • Unhurried time at the hot springs and caves
  • Both crater rims and the rainforest descent

Included

  • All meals on the mountain (vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free on request)
  • Drinking water, tea, coffee and snacks

Itinerary

Day 0 — Arrival

Pickup and transfer to Sembalun. Briefing and optional village stay.

Day 1 — Sembalun → Sembalun Crater Rim

Savanna crossing and climb to the rim. About 7–8 hours.

Day 2 — Summit → Segara Anak Lake

Summit at sunrise, breakfast at camp, then descend into the caldera. Afternoon at the hot springs, camp by the lake. About 9–10 hours.

Day 3 — Lake → Senaru Crater Rim

Slow morning at the lake, then climb to the Senaru rim for sunset. About 3–4 hours.

Day 4 — Senaru Crater Rim → Senaru

Sunrise, breakfast and the forest descent to Senaru. Transfer to your hotel. About 5 hours.

FAQ

How high is Mount Rinjani?

Mount Rinjani rises to 3,726 m, making it the second-highest volcano in Indonesia and the highest point on Lombok.

When is the trekking season?

The national park is usually open from April to December. It closes during the peak of the rainy season (roughly January to March) for safety and environmental recovery.

How fit do I need to be?

The crater rim trek suits anyone with regular exercise habits. Summit treks are demanding: expect long, steep sections on loose volcanic sand. If you can hike 6–8 hours on consecutive days, you are ready.

Can I customise my trek?

Yes. Every itinerary can be adjusted: private groups, extra nights at the lake, transport around Lombok or a pre-trek stay in the village. Message us and we will build it with you.

How do I book?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form with your dates and group size. We confirm availability, then a small deposit secures your spot; the balance is paid on arrival.
